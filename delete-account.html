Request account deletion

Dalhousie Companion
Last updated: September 19, 2026

To request deletion of your Dalhousie Companion account and associated personal data, email dfiagbe@gmail.com from the email address used for your account.

Include these details

  • Your account email address and name.
  • A clear request to delete your Dalhousie Companion account.
  • Any specific programme records or uploaded documents you want reviewed for deletion.

What happens next

The programme administrator will verify the request, disable the account, and delete personal account data and associated uploads where retention is not required. Records may be retained when necessary for programme audit, security, backup, legal, or continuity requirements. We will respond to the request using the requesting account email.

For general privacy information, read the Privacy Policy.
