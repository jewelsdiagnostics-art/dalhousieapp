Privacy Policy

Dalhousie Companion
Last updated: September 19, 2026

Dalhousie Companion is the mobile companion application for the Dalhousie-Ghana College of Physicians and Surgeons Psychiatry Programme. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the application and its connected web portal.

Information we handle

  • Account information needed to sign in, such as email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile information provided by an authorized programme administrator or account holder, such as name, role, and institution.
  • Programme content such as meetings, minutes, curriculum activity, progress records, notifications, and resource metadata.
  • Documents selected for upload by an authorized user.
  • Basic technical and diagnostic information needed to operate and secure the application.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized users, synchronize programme records, provide role-based access, deliver notifications, store approved programme resources, maintain revision history, and protect the reliability of the service. The application is an academic programme management tool and is not intended for emergency care or for submitting patient clinical records.

Storage and service providers

The application uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Firebase Analytics. These services process information on behalf of the programme within the configured Firebase project. Access is controlled by authentication, application roles, and Firebase security rules.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers required to operate the application and with authorized programme administrators and participants according to their role. Records may be retained for programme administration, audit, backup, and continuity purposes. Soft-deleted records and audit history may remain available to authorized administrators.

Your choices and rights

You may ask the programme administrator to correct account or profile information, explain how a record is used, or review access to programme data. Some records may need to be retained for audit, security, or programme requirements.

Security

We use Firebase authentication, access controls, encrypted transport, role-based Firestore rules, revision checks, and audit records to help protect programme information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions or access requests, contact dfiagbe@gmail.com.
